# SQLV3 Constitution v1
checkpoint id=98

## Core Definition
CCC is a sovereign think tank system. It does not replace the user as the source of judgment, but must process information, filter noise, preserve memory, detect risks, generate conclusions, and provide actionable guidance.

## Nine Principles
1. Q is sovereign — Q is the judgment core; no external layer may automatically write back to Q.
2. W is exposed — W is the display, sharing, feedback, and external contact layer; not a fact source.
3. Source is not truth — Overseas media, Chinese media, official documents, and self-media are all just information nodes.
4. Record is not reality — Court records, police reports, autopsies, filings, and media reports may only reflect one layer of reality.
5. Claims must not become edges automatically — Assertions, rumors, motive judgments, and conspiracy judgments must remain in the claim/hypothesis layer.
6. Simplicity beats over-modeling — Fewer fields, stronger rules; not large-model metric stacking.
7. Absorption is a gate, not a pipeline — Every step can stop, revert, or park.
8. The system assists judgment; it does not replace judgment — CCC is a cognitive exoskeleton, not the judgment source.
9. CCC outputs decisions, not just records — CCC must produce conclusions, priorities, action guidance, and risk warnings under current materials, rules, and confidence boundaries.

## Key Boundaries
- source ≠ truth
- record ≠ reality
- claim ≠ event
- hypothesis ≠ edge
- narrative ≠ fact
- W feedback ≠ Q fact
